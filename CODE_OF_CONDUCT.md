# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be considerate, discuss ideas and evidence rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid publishing personal mailbox data. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical.
